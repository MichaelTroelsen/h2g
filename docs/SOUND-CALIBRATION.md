# Sound calibration

Generated by `python/sound_calibrate.py` (h2g 0.5.597, 70e9bf0-dirty), 180 s renders. **FAIL** -- nothing downstream may inherit an approval on these numbers.

The window is 180 s because `approvals.py` compares renders at whatever window `build/approvals.json` was generated at, and a floor measured at any other window is a floor about a different quantity -- re-run this calibration at the window `approvals.py` currently uses, not at a convenient one.

**Checks 2, 3 and 4 are scored over the first 60 s of the aligned 180 s render** (`CHECK_WINDOW_S`; the alignment is found on the whole render and only the scoring is cut). `aud` and `loud` are means over frames, so a defect of fixed length is diluted in proportion to the window it is averaged into, and a margin that shrinks as the render grows is a fact about the render length, not the metric. Each table below carries the whole-window figure beside the prefix one; the prefix decides. Over the whole render the two floors read noise `0.1187` and closeness `0.9761` -- `approvals.py` applies the floors over the whole window, so those are the ones to read beside its verdicts.

The two numbers every decision uses, measured here and typed nowhere:

* **noise floor** = `0.1187` -- the largest of three: a 3 ms (48 rasterline) or 20 ms (one frame) delay of one side (`0.0021`, the figure this file used to call the floor), a delay of exactly half a hop (`64` samples; with the first two, `0.0095`), and **a second render of the same original against the cached one (`0.1187` over 28 repeat renders of 60 s)**. The last is the render's own reproducibility -- sidplayfp's power-on delay is random unless `--delay=<n>` is passed, and `listen.render_sidplayfp` does not pass it -- and it is the floor under every score taken against a cached render. It is a maximum over samples of a random start state, so it is a lower bound that rises as runs accumulate (`sound.REPEAT_KEEP` renders per tune are kept in build/audio across runs); the remedy is the renderer's flag, not a wider floor (`sound.render_repeat`). A change smaller than this is not a change. **Adopting it loses a pair the grid floor saw: `5_Title_Tunes.sid`** -- read that row of check 4 as a margin inside the render's noise, not as a blind spot.
* **closeness floor** = `0.9769893881351414` -- the least agreement between two renders a listener called the same (check 3). A build at least this close to an approved render sounds like what was approved.

## 6. Known-bad clocks

A per-frame timbre/level agreement is **blind to a clock**: a build at the wrong tempo or multiplier plays the same sounds at the wrong times, so `aud` and `loud` are never asked about these pairs. They are read on `len` (seconds ours ends past the original's ending, +-5 s is the rule) and `drift` (frames per 1000), built through each tree's own `convert(**presets)` -- the path FIDELITY.md measures -- because the CLI drops `skip_gate`.
