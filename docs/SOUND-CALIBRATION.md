Each table below carries the whole-window figure beside the prefix one; the prefix decides. * **noise floor** = `0.0018` -- the largest movement of `aud`/`loud` under a 3 ms (48 rasterline) and a 20 ms (one frame) delay of one side. A change smaller than this is not a change.
